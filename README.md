# OneSelf

A weekly check-in app. Plan the week's tasks, track screen time, look back at the week, and see how you are doing over time.

**Live site:** https://ydesai877.github.io/weekly-check/

## What it does

- **Home.** See the week at a glance: task progress, screen time against your daily limit, a mood check, a daily check-in, and an activity heat map.
- **Task deck.** Your open tasks show as swipe cards. Swipe up to complete a task. Swipe down to keep it for later. Swipe left or right to see other tasks. Arrow keys work too.
- **Add a task.** Set the category, Home or Outside (with travel time), Urgency, Effort and Impact, an optional time, and how often it repeats.
- **Structures.** Save a set of tasks, such as a gym plan or a month-end reset. Add the whole set to the week in one tap. Edit, delete, or reset the default structures.
- **Carry over.** Move last week's open tasks into this week, or skip them for good.
- **Screen time.** Set a daily goal. See your weekly ring, daily bars, a forecast, and a summary. Travel time counts 1 hour as 5 minutes.
- **Stats.** View tasks completed, the screen-time championship, mood, focus areas, gratitude, and energy by week, month, quarter, or year.
- **Streaks and countdown.** Track weeks in a row, best run, days of check-ins, and a custom countdown of up to 120 days.
- **Weekly pages.** Preview the week, plan tasks, log screen time, review, and list your wins.
- **Timeline.** Open any past week to read it or edit it.
- **Demo mode.** Try the app with 25 sample weeks. It keeps its data apart from your own project.

## Privacy

Your data stays in your browser. The app has no server and no account. Nothing is sent anywhere. Clearing your browser data removes your weeks.
